## Overview

**OneSoul e-Corner — Vision 2026** is a next-generation digital commerce platform built on Next.js 15 with Turbopack and powered by the full Firebase suite. It goes far beyond a typical storefront — combining real-time product commerce, a TipTap-powered news hub, a live jobs portal, vlog and e-book distribution, AI-powered content workflows via Google Genkit, and a protected admin dashboard into one unified, cyberpunk-aesthetic platform.

### The Challenge
Building a unified platform ecosystem — shop, content, jobs, services, and media — that feels premium on every screen, stays real-time, and remains manageable by a single admin without sacrificing performance or developer ergonomics.

### The Solution
Architected as a feature-rich Next.js 15 App Router application with clearly isolated feature domains, each backed by Firestore for real-time data. A TipTap-powered admin CMS handles content authoring, Google Genkit drives AI features, and Firebase Auth guards the entire back-office.

## Key Features
- 🛍️ **Shop + Wishlist + Cart**: Real-time Firestore-synced commerce engine.
- 📰 **News Hub**: TipTap rich-text articles authored and published via the admin dashboard.
- 💼 **Jobs Portal**: Live career listings synced from Firestore in real time.
- 🤖 **AI Integration**: Google Genkit for generative content and dynamic loading.
- ⌘K **Command Search**: Global keyboard-driven search across the platform.
- 🔐 **Admin Dashboard**: Firebase Auth-protected CRUD back-office for every domain.
- 🎥 **Vlogs & E-Books**: Dedicated multimedia and digital product distribution.
